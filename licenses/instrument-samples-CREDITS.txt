Files / original source as catalogued by upstream:
- piano/A3.mp3, piano/A4.mp3: VSCO 2 Community Edition (VSO2 in upstream source notes), Versilian Studios. Original library recordings by Sam Gossner and Simon Dalzell; sample cutting by Elan Hickler / Soundemote.
- flute/C4.mp3, flute/C5.mp3: same VSCO 2 Community Edition source.
- violin/A3.mp3, violin/A4.mp3: same VSCO 2 Community Edition source. Used for the app's solo-violin / strings option, not a full recorded string ensemble.
- guitar-acoustic/C3.mp3, guitar-acoustic/C4.mp3: University of Iowa Electronic Music Studios musical instrument samples (Iowa in upstream source notes).

Upstream sample processing and compilation: Nathaniel Brosowsky / tonejs-instruments contributors. Upstream documents trimming, ramps, volume matching, normalization, noise removal, and occasional pitch correction.
Upstream source record is retained as instrument-samples-upstream-sources.txt.

Changes for this app: MP3 decoded into mono PCM16 at 22,050 Hz, capped at eight seconds per anchor, peak gain adjusted by at most 2x toward 26000/32768, final 100 ms faded out, embedded as base64 in samples.js. No loops were baked into the embedded PCM. During playback the renderer skips leading codec padding and creates phase-aligned, 65 ms crossfaded sustain loops for long flute and violin notes. Other notes are pitch-shifted from the nearest recorded anchor, which changes timbre/duration; this is a compact starter instrument set rather than a full professional sound library.

The original download manifest is not present in this source snapshot. Included PCM hashes, lengths and note mappings are recorded in docs/embedded-samples.json. Source identities above are inherited from the retained upstream attribution records; no new verification of original MP3 bytes is claimed.
